# Floor 2 video and LiDAR reconstruction

This is the reproducible source for the replacement default Floor 2 model. Scan `CA3352E3-7C9E-465C-A643-CADF1385B3C9` supplies approximately 351.5 seconds of video, 3,515 frame records and 3,508 depth records. All 36 segments were sampled at roughly 0.9-second intervals, using only finite depth from 0.25–8 meters with ARKit confidence 2. The resulting structural evidence contains 1.55 million points from 381 camera frames. Lower-confidence narrow railing returns are explicitly identified in the furnishing evidence and checked visually.

The reviewed evidence and source manifest remain tracked. The reconstruction specification and preparation/navigation validation reports are generated locally and ignored by Git. Historical preview images and post-export receipts are omitted.

`manifest.json` records exact hashes of the five supplied files and the preserved routing map/scan scene. The 935 MiB source video archive remains external; it is not added to the application or repository. `source-geometry.json` retains the completed capture geometry, observed footprints, and camera walk without imagery or raw depth buffers.

`registration.json` gives the rigid scan-to-existing-map transform and residuals. Two adjoining lounge walls fit at 4.97 cm endpoint RMS; independent nearby walls agree within 9 cm. The older and newer balcony measurements disagree locally by 0.2–1 meters, so newer structure replaces the previous balcony illustration. The earlier measured lounge geometry completes local surfaces and furniture that the final RoomPlan state omitted. The model retains local AR uncertainty rather than claiming survey accuracy.

`structural-evidence.json` contains deduplicated walls supported by depth. `structural-repairs.json` records three depth-supported fixes to truncated segments. `furnishing-evidence.json` and `cafe-evidence.json` contain video frame times, pixel observations, paired depth positions, dimensional estimates and uncertainty for the additional furnishings and glazing. `floor-evidence.json` is the measured floor-support outline before cleanup.

`reconstruction.json` is the final compact model specification. It contains one continuous floor with nonoverlapping tile and carpet regions, cleaned wall segments, measured door cuts, open balustrades, glazing, and simplified furnishings. Furniture silhouettes, occluded floor boundaries and cutaway heights are styled; the vertical floor drift is flattened. Isolated wall fragments inside unentered rooms are excluded. Source data remains available to review every such choice.

Furniture `dimensions_m` records width, depth and a height reference. For the orange sofa and modular seating islands, the third value is the observed seating surface; the styled backs reach about 0.81 m and 1 m respectively. Other template dimensions define a target envelope, and their visible silhouette can be smaller. In particular, the planter template occupies about 84% of its catalog width and depth. Navigation uses the observed horizontal footprint envelopes before visual template scaling, with route clearance applied once. Source measurement uncertainty remains in the furnishing evidence.

`preparation-validation.json` records floor area, observed support, continuity and geometry counts. Preparation also checks that furnishings and walls are supported by the floor. Native export and final bundle validation are recorded in `../floors-2-3/validation.json` and `../floors-2-3/glb-validation.json`.

The lounge doorway includes a measured threshold completion between its jambs. This closes a roughly 2 cm seam between the two captures' floor outlines. `navigation-validation.json` describes the separately generated routing map. It combines confidence-2 depth support with valid earlier floor measurements, permits a 35 cm cleanup allowance around observed depth, and clips the result to the reconstructed floor. A 7.5 cm raster margin prevents corner clipping; the exposed balcony edge has a 52.5 cm inset. Reconciled walls, railings, glazing, observed furniture and measured doorways replace obsolete drifting geometry. This extends routing through the captured gallery, restroom passage and west seating. Rebuild with `scripts/build-floor-2-navigation.py`; its optional `--route-results` argument checks native AccessCore paths against the exact generated map and floor. `Floor2RoutingTests` checks routes through this network against the actual bundled USDZ floor triangles, with no fallback through unmapped space.

`localization-source-map.json` is the map paired with the subsequently supplied ARWorldMap. The archive is bundled as `student-center-east-floor-2.arworldmap`, with its hash and rigid map registration recorded in the adjacent localization JSON. Its capture geometry and timestamps match the reconstruction's source. Session relocalization still requires physical-device testing at the venue.

Reproduce the prepared specification with `scripts/prepare-video-lidar-floor-2.py` (Python, NumPy, Shapely >=2.1), then export and render with Blender using `scripts/build-video-lidar-floor-2.py`. The Blender build applies the inverse existing app registration so the model displays at scan scale. The model builder does not create routing coverage, clearance, an AR world map, or inter-floor connections. The separate navigation and localization resources described above provide those supported capabilities. Floor 3, the original measured routing map, and the cached Floor scan are preserved.
