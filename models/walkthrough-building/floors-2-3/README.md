# Floors 2 and 3

Floor 2 is a clean reconstruction of the supplied synchronized video and LiDAR scan `CA3352E3-7C9E-465C-A643-CADF1385B3C9`. It includes the lounge, atrium balconies, gallery, restroom passage, and west seating area. Pale cutaway walls, blue-gray furniture and open metal railings keep it readable at map scale. Floor 3 remains the earlier estimated video reconstruction, unchanged.

All files listed below are generated locally and ignored by Git. After rebuilding, open `preview.html` for the interactive model, with floor selection, orbit, pan, zoom, and top view. Floor 2 is selected by default. The app uses its separate bundled `student-center-east-floor-2-model.usdz` as its default Floor 2 model.

The recording contains 3,515 camera frames and 3,508 synchronized depth records over about 351.5 seconds. The structural fit uses high-confidence depth sampled across the entire walk, RoomPlan walls and openings, plus previous measured lounge geometry where the final scan dropped surfaces. Video identifies furnishings and materials; paired depth constrains their placement. Original source hashes and the compact, reproducible specification are in `../floor-2-reconstruction/`.

The floor silhouette is cleaned under furniture; furniture shapes and cutaway heights are styled. Local tracking drift remains, and the floor is flattened. The atrium and unobserved interiors are not filled in. The original routing map and Floor scan layer are preserved; the visual model does not establish new walking coverage, clearances, AR localization, or connections to Floor 3. The scan fit at the lounge is rigid, with 4.97 cm endpoint RMS; its agreement is local, not a survey accuracy claim for the entire floor.

The existing app registration maps authored coordinates back to scan meters. Exports are Y-up, Floor 2 at zero, Floor 3 at an estimated 4 meters. Blender source is Z-up. Lighting and the studio background are preview-only. Video imagery, people and personal belongings are not embedded.

## Rebuild

From the repository root, use Python with NumPy and Shapely 2.1 or newer, and Blender 4.5:

```sh
python3 scripts/prepare-video-lidar-floor-2.py
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python scripts/build-video-lidar-floor-2.py -- --samples 24
python3 scripts/build-walkthrough-floor-viewer.py
cp models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/floor-2.usdz InView/Resources/StudentCenter/student-center-east-floor-2-model.usdz
cp models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/floor-2.png InView/Resources/StudentCenter/student-center-east-floor-2-model.png
cp models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/layout.json InView/Resources/StudentCenter/student-center-east-model-layout.json
python3 scripts/validate-floor-models.py --check-app-resources
```

The preparation step rebuilds the specification from retained evidence without reprocessing the original 935 MiB video archive. The Blender builder recreates the output directory and restores unchanged Floor 3 exports from `../floors-2-3-video.zip`. That required baseline archive retains only the seven source members used by the builders and validator, with each member's original bytes preserved. The legacy builder needs `illustration-floors.json`, regenerated by `scripts/build-student-center-illustration-floors.swift` after running the evidence merge.
