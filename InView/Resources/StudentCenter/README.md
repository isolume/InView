# Student Center East assets

`student-center-east-floor-2.json` combines the supplied `venue-map 3.json` with the new exterior-lounge scan `E3CF408C-680C-4ECF-B9EB-F09117308BD3`. It keeps the original scan coordinates, all 77 original footprints, 12 measured floor regions and both elevator markers. It adds 19 completed walls, one door and one opening along the balcony-to-restroom route, for 98 footprints total. It represents a scanned area, not complete building coverage. Floor 1 has its separate measured routing map described below; Floor 3 remains display-only.

Floor 1 combines seven registered recordings in `student-center-east-floor-1-model.usdz`. The auditorium's raised lobby remains part of **Floor 1**, with its physical elevation retained. `student-center-east-floor-1-navigation.json` contains separate measured surfaces joined by explicit steps and elevator connections. Step-free routes use the elevator to reach the raised lobby. The distinct food court dining terrace has a video- and depth-confirmed sloped corridor; this is not a ramp to the auditorium lobby. Arbitrary starts and destinations can be picked on measured surfaces, and route pins and segments retain their elevations.

The Floor 1 visual model completes occluded floor patches, including the food court, while retaining the atrium opening. Completed appearance does not create navigation coverage. The auditorium foyer is a routing destination; the internal seating rake, stage and unverified dark corners remain visual-only. Floor 1 currently supports map route previews; the independent saved AR sessions do not yet establish a shared on-site localization session. Source audit, registration, visual evidence, reconstruction and navigation receipts are retained in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-1-reconstruction/`.

The app routes using `student-center-east-floor-2-navigation.json`. `scripts/build-floor-2-navigation.py` combines the registered LiDAR floor support with valid prior measurements, clipped to the reconstructed floor outline. Reconciled walls, railings, glazing, observed furniture and measured passages replace the drifting older balcony geometry. This supplies routes through the captured gallery, restroom passage and west seating while preserving the open atrium and floor-edge clearance. The original routing source above remains unchanged for reproducibility; both elevator points and the twelve measured lounge obstacles are retained exactly.

`student-center-east-floor-2.arworldmap` is the saved ARKit archive supplied for scan `CA3352E3-7C9E-465C-A643-CADF1385B3C9`. Its paired `venue-map.json` is retained as `floor-2-reconstruction/localization-source-map.json`. All 24 walls, 6 doors and 5 obstacles match the recording's processed scan; archive timestamps match the recording's end. `student-center-east-floor-2-localization.json` binds the archive and navigation map by hash and records the same rigid transform used by the model builder. After ARKit relocalizes, the session origin is moved into the canonical map frame before any position or AR route is shown. Startup and returning to Floor 2 both load these assets. Published geometry can use them only when it matches the bundled navigation geometry; incompatible releases do not silently replace the default map. Actual relocalization must be tested on a supported device at the venue.

The default Floor 2 3D model is the clean video-and-LiDAR reconstruction from scan `CA3352E3-7C9E-465C-A643-CADF1385B3C9`. It covers the revisited lounge, atrium balconies, gallery, restroom spur, and west seating. The synchronized 351.5-second recording contains 3,515 video frames and 3,508 depth records. High-confidence depth, completed RoomPlan geometry, and retained measurements in the revisited lounge constrain the structure; paired video/depth observations place the additional benches, fountains, modular seating, planter, column, and café furnishings.

Floor 2 has a continuous, nonoverlapping floor surface with carpet and tile regions, clean cutaway walls, door openings, and open metal balustrades. Furniture silhouettes, occluded floor edges, and wall heights are simplified; the floor is flattened to remove local vertical tracking drift. Unobserved room interiors and the atrium remain empty. Source hashes, structural evidence, furniture observations, rigid registration, and the editable reconstruction specification live in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-2-reconstruction/`. Video images and people are not embedded in the assets.

The scan is rigidly registered at two shared lounge walls, with a 4.97 cm endpoint RMS and independent lounge checks within 9 cm. Local disagreement is larger along the long balcony; the navigation resource therefore uses the reconciled structure and clips all floor coverage to the displayed outline. The asset is authored through the inverse of the existing `StudentCenterModelAlignment.swift` transform, retaining both elevator anchors and the shared asset frame. Floor 2 is at Y = 0; Floor 3 remains at its approximate Y = 4 meters and is byte-for-byte unchanged.

`student-center-east-floor-2-model.usdz` is loaded by the default Model layer. Its PNG and the shared layout metadata match the exports in `models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/`. The original measured map and cached Floor scan layer remain available as source evidence.

Layout labels have stable IDs derived from their floor and title. Floor 2 labels can be selected for directions through the captured floor network; a label alone cannot create walkable coverage. They do not identify verified restroom entrances. Floor 3 labels remain display-only. `estimatedScene(for:)` supplies an empty canvas source containing no measured surfaces, objects or floor coverage, permitting Floor 3 browsing while its measured map remains absent.

`student-center-east-floor-2-scene.json` is prepared through `VenueScene`, world-outline-to-local-polygon conversion, `IndoorFloorContent` and `FinalMapSceneBuilder`, reproducible with `scripts/build-student-center-scene.swift`. It retains map coordinates and provides a cutaway without rebuilding furniture on first launch. The map stays authoritative for routing and annotations. The revised cache has 82 surfaces and 16 display objects, with no raw mesh or point-cloud layer.

For the Floor scan layer, each object uses one simple measured box instead of baked furniture templates. Source IDs, categories, poses, measured dimensions, obstacle corners and obstacle bounds are preserved, as are all measured surfaces and floor outlines. This display-only simplification makes the initial map lightweight; the alternative USDZ layer supplies the detailed visual reconstruction.

The four source files from the earlier E3CF408C scan and its preceding map are preserved exactly in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-2-evidence/`. Its manifest records the rigid scan fit (3.17 cm endpoint RMS; independent wall checks within 8.85 cm), hashes and individual merge decisions. Overlapping completed measurements retain the earlier structure. Raw walls are alignment evidence only; raw obstacles, the camera walk and broad floor envelopes do not create new walkable coverage. Those earlier files contained no saved ARWorldMap or geographic registration; localization now uses the separately supplied archive described above. Elevator labels do not establish verified connections to other floors.

The native catalog also validates imported or published maps before rendering. It accepts building-scale geometry with finite, bounded coordinates, a maximum 500-meter map span, unique identified features and nondegenerate floor outlines. Geometry beyond those limits requires partitioning into smaller floor maps.
