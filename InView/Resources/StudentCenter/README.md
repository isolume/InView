# Student Center East assets

`student-center-east-floor-2.json` combines the supplied `venue-map 3.json` with the new exterior-lounge scan `E3CF408C-680C-4ECF-B9EB-F09117308BD3`. It keeps the original scan coordinates, all 77 original footprints, 12 measured floor regions and both elevator markers. It adds 19 completed walls, one door and one opening along the balcony-to-restroom route, for 98 footprints total. It represents a scanned area, not complete building coverage. Floors 1 and 3 have no bundled measured routing maps.

The visual resources preserve the exported assets from `models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/`. Floor 2 now uses the registered scan structure and the earlier scan's measured furniture placements, with styled furniture silhouettes and shortened cutaway walls. Its native exterior floor envelope appears only in the illustration; it is not added to routing coverage. The three broad observed floor additions are excluded from both the revised illustration and routing. Floor 3 remains the original estimated video reconstruction, byte-for-byte unchanged. Closed room interiors, unscanned Floor 2 wings and Floor 1 are not modeled. The original two-floor video model is preserved in `models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3-video.zip`.

Both floors retain the existing shared asset frame and elevator registration in `StudentCenterModelAlignment.swift`. Floor 2 is authored through the inverse of that registration, so applying the existing display transform reproduces its saved map coordinates and measured dimensions. The fixed anchor locations `(-1.30, 0, 8.82)` / `(1.07, 0, 8.82)` now represent the original elevator markers in that frame. Floor 2 is at Y = 0 and Floor 3 retains its estimated Y = 4 meters. Floor 3 and the relationship between floors remain approximate; no measured floor connection or live AR alignment is established.

Layout labels are display-only landmarks with stable IDs derived from their floor and title. Floor 2 labels follow the scanned lounge, lifts, balcony and restroom passage; they do not identify verified restroom entrances or become routing access points. `estimatedScene(for:)` supplies an empty canvas source containing no measured surfaces, objects or floor coverage, permitting Floor 3 browsing while its measured map remains absent.

`student-center-east-floor-2-scene.json` is prepared through `VenueScene`, world-outline-to-local-polygon conversion, `IndoorFloorContent` and `FinalMapSceneBuilder`, reproducible with `scripts/build-student-center-scene.swift`. It retains map coordinates and provides a cutaway without rebuilding furniture on first launch. The map stays authoritative for routing and annotations. The revised cache has 82 surfaces and 16 display objects, with no raw mesh or point-cloud layer.

For the Floor scan layer, each object uses one simple measured box instead of baked furniture templates. Source IDs, categories, poses, measured dimensions, obstacle corners and obstacle bounds are preserved, as are all measured surfaces and floor outlines. This display-only simplification makes the initial map lightweight; the alternative USDZ layer supplies the detailed visual reconstruction.

All four new source files and the earlier map are preserved exactly in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-2-evidence/`. Its manifest records the rigid scan fit (3.17 cm endpoint RMS; independent wall checks within 8.85 cm), hashes and individual merge decisions. Overlapping completed measurements retain the earlier structure. Raw walls are alignment evidence only; raw obstacles, the camera walk and broad floor envelopes do not create new walkable coverage. The supplied map has no saved ARWorldMap or geographic registration. Elevator labels do not establish verified connections to other floors.

The native catalog also validates imported or published maps before rendering. It accepts building-scale geometry with finite, bounded coordinates, a maximum 500-meter map span, unique identified features and nondegenerate floor outlines. Geometry beyond those limits requires partitioning into smaller floor maps.
