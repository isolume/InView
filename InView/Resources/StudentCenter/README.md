# Student Center East assets

`student-center-east-floor-2.json` combines the supplied `venue-map 3.json` with the new exterior-lounge scan `E3CF408C-680C-4ECF-B9EB-F09117308BD3`. It keeps the original scan coordinates, all 77 original footprints, 12 measured floor regions and both elevator markers. It adds 19 completed walls, one door and one opening along the balcony-to-restroom route, for 98 footprints total. It represents a scanned area, not complete building coverage. Floors 1 and 3 have no bundled measured routing maps.

The default Floor 2 3D model is the clean video-and-LiDAR reconstruction from scan `CA3352E3-7C9E-465C-A643-CADF1385B3C9`. It covers the revisited lounge, atrium balconies, gallery, restroom spur, and west seating. The synchronized 351.5-second recording contains 3,515 video frames and 3,508 depth records. High-confidence depth, completed RoomPlan geometry, and retained measurements in the revisited lounge constrain the structure; paired video/depth observations place the additional benches, fountains, modular seating, planter, column, and café furnishings.

Floor 2 has a continuous, nonoverlapping floor surface with carpet and tile regions, clean cutaway walls, door openings, and open metal balustrades. Furniture silhouettes, occluded floor edges, and wall heights are simplified; the floor is flattened to remove local vertical tracking drift. Unobserved room interiors and the atrium remain empty. Source hashes, structural evidence, furniture observations, rigid registration, and the editable reconstruction specification live in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-2-reconstruction/`. Video images and people are not embedded in the assets.

The scan is rigidly registered at two shared lounge walls, with a 4.97 cm endpoint RMS and independent lounge checks within 9 cm. Local disagreement is larger along the long balcony; this is a visual reconstruction, not a new routing or localization result. The asset is authored through the inverse of the existing `StudentCenterModelAlignment.swift` transform, retaining both elevator anchors and the shared asset frame. Floor 2 is at Y = 0; Floor 3 remains at its approximate Y = 4 meters and is byte-for-byte unchanged.

`student-center-east-floor-2-model.usdz` is loaded by the existing default Model layer. Its PNG and the shared layout metadata match the exports in `models/walkthrough-building/floors-2-3/`. The separate measured routing map and cached Floor scan layer are unchanged.

Layout labels are display-only landmarks with stable IDs derived from their floor and title. Floor 2 labels follow the lounge, lifts, balconies, gallery, restroom passage and seating areas; they do not identify verified restroom entrances or become routing access points. `estimatedScene(for:)` supplies an empty canvas source containing no measured surfaces, objects or floor coverage, permitting Floor 3 browsing while its measured map remains absent.

`student-center-east-floor-2-scene.json` is prepared through `VenueScene`, world-outline-to-local-polygon conversion, `IndoorFloorContent` and `FinalMapSceneBuilder`, reproducible with `scripts/build-student-center-scene.swift`. It retains map coordinates and provides a cutaway without rebuilding furniture on first launch. The map stays authoritative for routing and annotations. The revised cache has 82 surfaces and 16 display objects, with no raw mesh or point-cloud layer.

For the Floor scan layer, each object uses one simple measured box instead of baked furniture templates. Source IDs, categories, poses, measured dimensions, obstacle corners and obstacle bounds are preserved, as are all measured surfaces and floor outlines. This display-only simplification makes the initial map lightweight; the alternative USDZ layer supplies the detailed visual reconstruction.

The four source files from the earlier E3CF408C scan and its preceding map are preserved exactly in `models/walkthrough-building/floor-2-evidence/`. Its manifest records the rigid scan fit (3.17 cm endpoint RMS; independent wall checks within 8.85 cm), hashes and individual merge decisions. Overlapping completed measurements retain the earlier structure. Raw walls are alignment evidence only; raw obstacles, the camera walk and broad floor envelopes do not create new walkable coverage. The supplied map has no saved ARWorldMap or geographic registration. Elevator labels do not establish verified connections to other floors.

The native catalog also validates imported or published maps before rendering. It accepts building-scale geometry with finite, bounded coordinates, a maximum 500-meter map span, unique identified features and nondegenerate floor outlines. Geometry beyond those limits requires partitioning into smaller floor maps.
